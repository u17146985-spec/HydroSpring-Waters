Frequently Asked Questions

What You Need to Know

What bottle sizes do you supply?

You can enquire about 330 ml, 500 ml, 1 litre, 1.5 litre and 5 litre bottled water. Other sizes and quantities can also be discussed on request. Please contact us to confirm availability and pricing.

How do I get a price?

Pricing is provided on quotation. Tell us your preferred bottle size, approximate quantity, label preference and delivery destination.

Can I order customised labels?

You can enquire about customised bottle labels for your business or event. We will discuss your requirements and confirm the available options.

Do you accept bulk and event enquiries?

You can submit enquiries for business supplies, conferences, church programmes and other events. Final availability and order arrangements must be confirmed.

Do you deliver throughout South Africa?

Nationwide enquiries may be considered depending on order size and destination. Contact us to confirm whether delivery is available for your specific order.

Does a quotation enquiry confirm my order?

No. An enquiry is the first step. Your order is confirmed only after the quotation, availability and arrangements have been agreed with HydroSpring Waters.
