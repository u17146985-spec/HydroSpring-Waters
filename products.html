Frequently Asked Questions

Before You Order

Which bottle sizes can I enquire about?

You can enquire about 330 ml, 500 ml, 1 litre, 1.5 litres, 5 litres and other sizes. Availability and minimum order quantities must be confirmed.

How much does the bottled water cost?

Pricing is available on quotation. Tell us the bottle size, quantity, label preference and destination so your requirements can be assessed.

Can I request customised bottle labels?

You can enquire about customised labels. Share your requirements so we can confirm the available options, costs and production arrangements.

Can I order water for an event?

You can request a quotation for church programmes, conferences, weddings and other functions. Share the event date, size and quantity you need.

Can you deliver outside Pretoria?

Enquiries from other locations may be considered depending on order size and destination. Delivery availability, cost and timing must be confirmed before an order is accepted.

Does completing the form place an order?

No. The form prepares a WhatsApp enquiry. Your order is not confirmed until you and HydroSpring Waters agree on the quotation and arrangements.
