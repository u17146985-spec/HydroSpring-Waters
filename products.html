Frequently Asked Questions

Before You Order

How much does the bottled water cost?

Pricing is available on quotation. Share your quantity, label preference and destination so the order can be assessed.

Can I request customised bottle labels?

You can enquire about customised labels. Share your requirements so we can confirm the available options.

Do you accept event orders?

You can request a quotation for church programmes, conferences, weddings and other functions. Availability and requirements must be confirmed for each order.

Can you deliver outside Pretoria?

Nationwide enquiries may be considered depending on order size and destination. Delivery availability, cost and timing must be confirmed before an order is accepted.

Does completing the form place an order?

No. The form prepares a WhatsApp enquiry. Your order is not confirmed until you and HydroSpring Waters agree on the quotation and arrangements.
